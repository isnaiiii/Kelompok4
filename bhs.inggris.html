How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Decorative Key Sphere

Paragraph 1 – General Statement

Electronic waste is a growing environmental issue caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they become outdated or broken. However, their plastic keycaps are durable materials that can be creatively upcycled. One unique way to reuse these components is by transforming them into a spherical decorative desk object.

Paragraph 2 – The Transformation Process
The transformation process begins by detaching the keycaps from an unused computer keyboard. The plastic keys are thoroughly cleaned to remove dust, dirt, and oil accumulated over time. Meanwhile, a spherical base object (such as a styrofoam or plastic ball) is prepared to serve as the core structure for the transformation.

Paragraph 3 – The Formation of a New Product
Next, the clean keycaps are attached to the spherical surface using strong adhesive. The keys are arranged systematically—often following grid patterns or keeping their character sequences—to cover the entire surface evenly. Once all keys are firmly secured and the glue has dried completely, the flat elements form a three-dimensional decorative sphere.

Paragraph 4 – The Result and Its Impact
a result, an obsolete computer keyboard is transformed into a unique aesthetic centerpiece for a desk or shelf. This creative process repurposes electronic waste, reduces solid rubbish, and promotes sustainable living through upcycling.

How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Night Lamp
Paragraph 1 – General Statement Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often thrown away when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, the keyboard keys can still be reused to make something useful. One creative way to reduce this waste is by transforming an old keyboard into a night lamp. This shows that unused materials can be turned into beautiful and useful products. Paragraph 2 – The Transformation Process The transformation begins by preparing an unused keyboard. The plastic keys are removed from the keyboard and separated from the other electronic parts. After that, the keys are cleaned to remove dust and dirt. The keys are then prepared to be used as decorative materials for the night lamp. Paragraph 3 – The Formation of a New Product The cleaned keyboard keys are arranged and attached to the frame of the night lamp. The keys are placed neatly to create an attractive design. A lighting component is added so the lamp can produce light. Different letters, numbers, and symbols on the keys create a unique appearance. After all the parts are assembled properly, the night lamp is ready to be used. Paragraph 4 – The Result and Its Impact As a result, an old computer keyboard is transformed into a night lamp that has both functional and aesthetic value. The lamp can be used as a bedroom decoration and provides soft light at night. This transformation helps reduce electronic waste and encourages people to reuse unused materials. Therefore, recycling old keyboards into night lamps is a creative way to support a cleaner and more sustainable environment.

How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Night Lamp
Paragraph 1 – General Statement

Electronic waste is one of the problems that can harm the environment. Old computer keyboards are often thrown away when they are broken or no longer used. However, some parts of the keyboard can still be reused to make something useful. One creative idea is to turn an old keyboard into a night lamp. This project shows how used electronic items can become a new and useful product.
Paragraph 2 – The Transformation Process
The process begins by collecting an unused keyboard and separating its parts. The keyboard keys are removed carefully from the main board. After that, the keys and other parts are cleaned to remove dust and dirt. The materials are then prepared to be used in making the night lamp.
Paragraph 3 – The Formation of a New Product
After the materials are ready, the keyboard keys are arranged around the lamp body to create an attractive design. The keys are attached one by one so they look neat and interesting. Then, the lighting component is placed inside the lamp. Once everything is properly arranged, the old keyboard is transformed into a unique night lamp.
Paragraph 4 – The Result and Its Impact
As a result, an old computer keyboard becomes a night lamp that can be used as a room decoration and a source of light. This product gives new value to unused materials and helps reduce electronic waste. It also shows that recycling can turn old objects into creative and useful products. Therefore, reusing old keyboards is one simple way to care for the environment.
